Senior Software Engineer · Bits Investigation

Current

Bits Investigation is Datadog's AI SRE. When an alert fires, it looks through the relevant metrics, logs, and traces and tries to explain what went wrong. I work on making it reliable enough for real incidents.

Taking Scout to production

Datadog recently released Scout 1.0, its first in-house model for investigating production issues. Scout was trained with reinforcement learning in simulated incident environments. I worked on putting it into production, integrating it with Bits Investigation so we could evaluate it on our actual production datasets and not just the environment it was trained in.

A model can do well in its training environment and still struggle with real alerts, messy telemetry, and the tools Bits calls in production. Testing on production data is how we find out.

Keeping the pipelines running

At this scale, some part of the system is usually failing. I worked on making the pipelines resilient to three kinds of problems:

  • Configuration: a bad push or misconfiguration shouldn't take processing down.
  • Data: malformed, unexpected, or skewed input shouldn't stop the pipeline.
  • Infrastructure: machine, network, and datacenter failures should slow processing, not stop it.

I also identified and mitigated race conditions in the live production systems themselves.

Cutting latency

My work brought average end-to-end latency down from about 24 hours to 6 hours, so teams that relied on these logs got their data about four times sooner.
